04 / A few answers

Good to know.

What can you help my business with?

We bring strategy, content, design and website development together. The right mix depends on your goals, audience and what is currently getting in the way.

How do we get started?

Send a short note about your business and what you would like to improve. We will take it from there with a conversation to understand your needs and see whether we are a good fit.

How long does a website project take?

Timing depends on the scope, content and feedback process. After we understand what you need, we can outline a realistic plan and key milestones before work begins.

Can you work with an existing website?

Yes. We can discuss a new direction or improvements to an existing site, depending on your priorities and the current setup.

Do you work with businesses outside Kerala?

Yes. We are based in Kerala and work with businesses in other locations too. We can collaborate remotely throughout the project.
